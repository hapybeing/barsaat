# Concepts considered (2026-10-11)

Scored 1–5 against what the Awwwards experience winners share (Messenger/abeto Dev SOTY 2025, Igloo Inc SOTY 2024, Lando Norris SOTY 2025):
a world you're *inside*, one tactile verb, sound, emotional payoff, and polish on every device.
Extra weights for Gaurang: unique to him, runs beautifully on an Android tablet in Brave, buildable to a high finish with procedural GL + one photo plate.

| Concept | Verb | Unique | Emotion | Tablet-native | Procedural strength | Risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Barsaat** — a fogged window on the Ganga in monsoon. Wipe, write, breathe on the glass; the storm comes and goes. | wipe / write / breathe | 5 | 5 | 5 (finger on glass *is* the device) | 5 (refraction, drops, fog, light) | 3 (rain-on-glass has prior art; must go far beyond it) | **23** |
| Murmuration — 16k starlings you herd with a finger at sunset | herd | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 (GPGPU on Mali) | 19 |
| Patang — Makar Sankranti kite duel on rooftops | fly / cut | 5 | 3 | 4 | 2 (needs modelled city) | 2 | 16 |
| Chhath — float diyas down a night river | place / release | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 (thin interaction) | 20 |
| Stele 2 (rework Bixi) | rake light | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 14 |

**Pick: Barsaat**, and it absorbs the best of Chhath: when the storm clears, diya lamps drift on the river.

Prior art I must beat, not copy: Lucas Bebber's "Rain & Water Effect" (Codrops 2015) and BigWings' "Heartfelt" shader. Both are effects. Barsaat is a place, a 3-minute weather story, a sound piece, and a UI that is written on the glass itself.
