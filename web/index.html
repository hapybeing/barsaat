बरसात

Barsaat

Every monsoon, children tear a page out of a school notebook, fold it into a boat and set it in the water running down the lane. This is that boat's whole night: the lane, the canal, the river in a storm, and the open water after.

How to play

  • Touch and hold to steer toward your finger. Keep holding to blow the boat along.
  • Steer through the dark lamps to light them. They follow you, and each one plays a note of Raag Megh, the raag of rain clouds.
  • Keyboard: ← → steer, space blows.

How it's made

Nothing here is a photo or a recording. The city, the river and the sky are generated as you go, in real-time WebGL with hand-written GLSL shaders: every raindrop leaves a ring on the water, every lamp breaks into a long streak in it, and the walls reflect with their lit windows. The boat is folded from the very page you wrote on. All the sound, from the rain on tin roofs to the tanpura, the thunder and the temple bells, is synthesised live with Web Audio.
